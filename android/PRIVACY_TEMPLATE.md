This app does not include a Maen analytics, advertising or account SDK and does
not send browsing history to a Maen service. Websites you visit receive ordinary
network requests and may process cookies, uploads, camera/microphone content or
other information you explicitly provide under their own policies.

The app stores website cookies/site data in the app-private Android WebView
profile. Saved page addresses and parental settings are encrypted locally using
Android Keystore. File selection is explicit through Android's document picker.
Camera/microphone require both site approval and Android permission. Downloads
use Android Download Manager; those files are stored outside app-private storage.

Android System WebView/Safe Browsing, Google Play and the operating system may
process security/reputation or diagnostic data under their provider's policies.
Do not label this as an unconditional claim that no data ever leaves the device.
The publisher must verify current provider behavior and complete Play Data Safety
accordingly. No camera/audio/files are collected by a Maen backend in this source.

You can clear cookies/site storage in Settings outside family mode, revoke
permissions in Android Settings, or delete the app's data/uninstall through
Android. Downloads already saved to shared storage require separate deletion.
Parental filtering is not a guarantee that all inappropriate content is blocked.
