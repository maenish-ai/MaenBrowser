Tabs & sessions

Settings

Choose a tab to switch to it. Release only discards safely idle pages.

Saved sessions

Only ordinary HTTP/HTTPS tabs are saved. Private tabs are excluded. Restore opens saved addresses; it does not restore unsaved form data.

Exports contain page addresses and may include private link tokens. Cookies and passwords are never exported.

Local recovery checkpoints

Automatic checkpoints keep web addresses locally, not unsaved forms or private tabs. Up to three checkpoints are retained. Unknown private link tokens may still appear in addresses; disable and delete checkpoints when needed.
